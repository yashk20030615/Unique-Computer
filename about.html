Who We Are

Panipat's Trusted Tech Partner

Unique Computer was established with a simple mission — to provide Panipat's residents and businesses with honest, quality computer services at fair prices. Over the years, we've grown into one of the most trusted names in the city.

From students buying their first laptop to businesses setting up entire computer labs — we've served thousands of customers across Panipat, building long-term relationships based on trust and quality.

Experienced Team: Our technicians have years of hands-on experience with all major laptop and desktop brands.

Genuine Parts: We only source original, certified spare parts — never cheap duplicates that fail early.

Local & Trusted: We're part of the Panipat community. Our reputation is our biggest asset.

Fair Pricing: Transparent, competitive pricing with no hidden charges — ever.
